# Cricket Greatness Lab — current analytical methodology

**Current public methodology: research v0.4.**

Read the [complete v0.4 scorecard-era methodology](format-greatness-methodology.md).

## Unified approach

The website now runs one parameterized method across Tests, ODIs and T20Is. All eras use the same scorecard fields: runs, dismissals, opposing nation, year, recorded venue, match identity and other batters' runs.

Our three headline dimensions are **era-adjusted quality**, **team carry**, and **peak versus career greatness**, including a clearly labeled experimental JAWS-inspired seven-best-season comparison. The quality model also incorporates shrunken opposition-, home/away- and same-match scoring proxies.

- **Tests + ODIs:** comparable within-format normalized scores for players eligible in both formats.
- **All three:** comparable normalized scores for players eligible in all three international formats. This excludes pre-T20I-era batters by eligibility, not zero imputation.
- **Modern detailed match explorer:** licensed Cricsheet records only, distinct from the all-era source-layer research.

The prior modern-only v0.2 ranking algorithm is **retired** from the website and no longer generated by its pipeline. Old formulas may be inspected in historical Git commits, but must not be mistaken for the current rankings.

### Important limitations

Not independently certified historical scorecard completeness; older individual bowling attacks and measurable pitches are not available for all eras. The ESPN-derived 2017 historical batting source has unverified redistribution rights; only derived aggregate player model scores are published in the new app. The JAWS analogue is not WAR and is opportunity-biased across eras.
