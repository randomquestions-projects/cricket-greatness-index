What is and is not controlled for?

Controls included in every era

Era: leave-player-out nearby-year scoring reference. Opposition: shrunken average scoring against the opposing nation, excluding the evaluated match. Home/away: estimated relative to other recorded home/away innings, when the venue host is known. Match difficulty: teammates and opponents in the same Test, excluding the batter's own appearances. Team carry: other batters in the same innings. All of these are based on scorecards, not modern delivery detail.

Separately modeled

Quality and team carry have match-cluster bootstrap 90% conditional intervals. Peak is a maximum rolling 25-innings adjusted-quality estimate; career greatness rewards both observed skill and cumulative adjusted production. Opposition, venue and match-condition controls can be individually removed in sensitivity checks on the player panels.

What remains unmeasured

Specific historical bowlers faced, consistent historic batting position, actual pitch measurements, weather, series identifiers, rules changes and many source gaps. Opponent and match scoring are imperfect proxies. Early Test competition pools were smaller and different from modern ones. Bootstrap intervals do not capture incomplete source coverage or model-choice uncertainty.
