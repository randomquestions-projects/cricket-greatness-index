# Open warehouse data license and provenance

Cricsheet data are distributed under the **Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY) v1.0**. See <https://cricsheet.org/licensing/> and <https://cricsheet.org/register/>. Data copyright and database rights, where applicable, remain with the relevant upstream holders. Redistribution of source snapshots and transformed event databases requires attribution.

Attribution for open warehouse:

> Cricket match and identity data provided by Cricsheet.org, under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY 1.0). Data transformed, normalized and partitioned by Cricket Master Warehouse.

Each versioned release retains the original licensed archive, data-source SHA256, output SHA256, and provenance manifest.

**Restricted sources are excluded.** Data published by Duncan Golicher derived from ESPNcricinfo, CricArcade, CricketArchive, Howstat, Cricket Database, ESPN Statsguru and ICC have not been established as unrestricted bulk-redistribution sources. Their appearance on a public website is not evidence of reusable-data rights. No raw records from these sources are incorporated into the open master release.

The separate legacy Greatest Batters website currently has its own 2017 historical innings derivatives, whose redistribution terms are **not fully verified**; those existing site assets need a separate rights review before the site is promoted or expanded. Building the open master warehouse does not resolve that issue.

No external historical full-scorecard source is asserted as open licensed until a source-specific data licence and usage conditions have been documented.
